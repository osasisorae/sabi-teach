# SabiTeach Demo Video Script

## Goal

Keep this under 3 minutes.

The tone should feel simple, honest, and grounded.
Do not oversell.
Do not claim fully on-device phone inference if that is not what the build is doing.

## Version 1 Script

### 0:00 - 0:20

Visual:

- open on the app home or generate screen
- show the SabiTeach name clearly
- quick cut to topic selection

Voiceover:

"This is SabiTeach. I’m building it because I’d genuinely like to see something like this exist in the real world.

The idea is simple: help a teacher turn one lesson topic into something usable in class, even in a low-connectivity environment."

### 0:20 - 0:45

Visual:

- show `Primary 5`
- show `English`
- show `Efik`
- tap a topic like `Nouns`

Voiceover:

"I’m not trying to build a giant edtech platform here. I’m starting with one very specific workflow.

Primary 5 English, with Efik as a support language.

A teacher picks a topic, taps generate, and gets a structured lesson they can actually use."

### 0:45 - 1:20

Visual:

- tap `Generate`
- show the loading state briefly
- cut into the generated lesson
- scroll through learning objective, teacher explanation, support explanation

Voiceover:

"The app generates a lesson objective, a teacher explanation in English, a support explanation in a local language, examples, a class activity, quiz questions, an answer key, and a take-home revision task.

The point is not to create a chatbot.

The point is to give a teacher something practical in a format that already feels like classroom work."

### 1:20 - 1:50

Visual:

- keep scrolling through quiz questions and answer key
- tap `Save Lesson`
- open `Saved`

Voiceover:

"That matters because a lot of tools assume perfect internet, laptops, subscriptions, and one learner sitting alone with a screen.

That is not the reality for a lot of classrooms here.

So I wanted this to feel small, direct, and useful."

### 1:50 - 2:20

Visual:

- open the saved lesson
- show it reopening cleanly
- if possible, briefly show network off or airplane mode during the saved-lesson view

Voiceover:

"Saved lessons stay on the phone, so the teacher can reopen them later without needing to generate everything again.

Right now, lesson generation is running locally through Gemma 4 via Ollama, and the saved lesson flow already works offline on the phone."

### 2:20 - 2:45

Visual:

- switch to a second topic like `Simple Sentence Structure`
- show a second generated lesson briefly

Voiceover:

"I kept the scope intentionally narrow.

One teacher.
One phone.
One subject first.
One support language first.

I’d rather build one honest thing that works than pretend I’m building everything at once."

### 2:45 - 3:00

Visual:

- end on the strongest lesson screen
- optionally show the logo / app name cleanly

Voiceover:

"This is still early, but the direction is clear.

If AI is going to help education here, it has to work in the environments where teachers are already doing the hardest work.

That’s what I’m building with SabiTeach."

## Delivery Notes

- Speak slowly.
- Leave short pauses between sections.
- Do not sound like a startup ad.
- Keep the screen recording steady and readable.
- If a generation step is slow, cut the wait in editing and resume on the result.
- Use the two strongest topics only.

## Best Demo Topics

- `Nouns`
- `Simple Sentence Structure`

## Claim Discipline

Safe claim:

- local Gemma 4 generation via Ollama
- saved lessons reopen offline on the phone

Avoid unless we actually build it:

- full on-device phone inference
- broad support for many Nigerian languages
- full curriculum coverage
