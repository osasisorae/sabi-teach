# Pitch And Story

## Core Story

A great teacher should not need perfect internet to prepare a great lesson.

SabiTeach gives teachers in low-connectivity Nigerian schools a practical AI copilot they can carry in one phone, helping them teach English with support from local Nigerian languages.

## Judge-Friendly Framing

This is not an abstract AI assistant.
This is a classroom tool for a specific real-world constraint:

- unreliable connectivity
- limited prep time
- limited teaching resources

## One-Sentence Video Hook

What if one Android phone could help a teacher teach English through Efik, Ibibio, Yoruba, Hausa, or another local language, even without internet?

## Three-Minute Video Structure

### Part 1: The Problem

Show:

- a teacher with limited time
- patchy or absent internet
- lesson prep done manually

Narration angle:

- millions of students depend on teachers working in constrained environments
- the teacher is capable, but the tools are wrong for the context

### Part 2: The Product

Show:

- subject and class selection
- support language selection
- topic entry
- one tap to generate lesson materials
- teacher reviewing outputs
- saved lesson reopened offline

Narration angle:

- this is built for the reality of low-connectivity classrooms
- Gemma 4 is generating practical bilingual teaching material, not just chatting

### Part 3: The Impact

Show:

- teacher using the lesson in class
- students answering quiz questions
- printed or copied revision sheet

Narration angle:

- better prepared lessons
- better student understanding
- real utility from local AI

## Writeup Spine

The Kaggle writeup should follow this structure:

1. The classroom problem
2. Why existing AI tools fail in this context
3. Our product approach
4. How Gemma 4 is used
5. Offline-first architecture
6. Demo workflow
7. Limitations and future work

## What Judges Should Remember

- clear social need
- strong offline relevance
- real working product
- explicit Gemma 4 usage
- emotionally credible story

## Demo Rule

The final demo should make the product understandable within the first 20 seconds.

If the viewer cannot explain what SabiTeach does after 20 seconds, the video is too complicated.
