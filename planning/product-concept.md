# Product Concept

## Working Name

SabiTeach

The name is temporary. The product direction is not.

## One-Sentence Pitch

SabiTeach is an offline-first Android teacher copilot that helps teachers in low-connectivity Nigerian schools turn a topic or textbook page into a usable lesson plan, explanation, quiz, and revision sheet with Gemma 4.

## Problem

Many teachers operate with:

- limited prep time
- unreliable internet
- few teaching materials
- overcrowded classrooms
- mixed student ability levels

Most AI education tools assume:

- always-on internet
- laptops
- paid subscriptions
- students learning individually

That is not the environment we are building for.

## User

Primary user:

- a teacher in a low-connectivity Nigerian school using an Android phone

Secondary users:

- students receiving printed or copied revision materials
- school operators or heads of school who want consistent lesson quality

## Core User Flow

1. The teacher selects class, subject, and topic.
2. The teacher either types a short prompt or snaps a textbook page.
3. Gemma 4 generates teaching materials aligned to the selected level.
4. The teacher reviews and edits if needed.
5. The teacher uses the output immediately in class or shares it with students.

## User Value

What the teacher gets:

- faster lesson preparation
- simpler explanations
- classroom-ready questions
- materials that still work offline

What the student gets:

- clearer explanations
- better revision support
- more consistent classroom quality

## Subject Strategy

Our first subject should be English.

Reason:

- English is the gateway subject for success across the rest of the curriculum.
- A teacher copilot that teaches English through local-language support is easy to understand and emotionally strong.
- It gives us a practical multilingual story without needing to solve every subject at once.

Core framing:

- teach English in a way that respects the learner's first language
- help teachers explain English concepts using familiar local language examples

## Language Strategy

We should not try to support every Nigerian language in the MVP.

Instead, we should build:

- one multilingual workflow
- a clean language-pack architecture
- two or three supported languages first

Recommended launch split:

- one major language for broad coverage
- one personal or locally authentic language for differentiation

Example:

- English + Yoruba
- English + Efik

or

- English + Hausa
- English + Ibibio

This is stronger than pretending to cover all Nigerian languages with shallow quality.

## Why This Can Win

- The problem is easy to understand in seconds.
- The impact is tangible and socially meaningful.
- The demo can show a real teacher workflow from input to classroom output.
- Gemma 4 is central to the product, not a thin wrapper.
- Offline-first design fits the hackathon theme directly.

## What Makes The Product Real

The MVP should prove these points:

- the app runs on a real mobile device
- the AI output is generated inside a real workflow
- the result is useful to a teacher the same day
- the system remains functional with poor or no internet

## Initial Product Promise

If you have one phone, one topic, and one class to teach, SabiTeach helps you prepare in minutes.
