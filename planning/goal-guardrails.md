# Goal Guardrails

## Our Actual Goal

Build a credible offline-first teacher copilot for low-connectivity Nigerian classrooms.

## What We Are Building

- a mobile-first teaching workflow
- one clear teacher use case
- one strong classroom demo
- a practical Gemma 4 application

## What We Are Not Building

- a full school management system
- a marketplace for courses
- a generic chatbot for everyone
- a student social network
- a broad LMS
- a startup pitch deck disguised as a product

## Scope Discipline

If a feature does not directly improve lesson preparation or in-class delivery, it is out.

If a feature cannot be shown clearly in the final demo, it is probably out.

If a feature requires heavy backend dependence, it is out unless it is essential.

## MVP Boundary

We only need enough functionality to prove:

- a teacher can input a topic
- Gemma 4 can produce grounded classroom materials
- the workflow works under low-connectivity assumptions
- English can be taught with local-language support in a real classroom workflow

## Product Principles

- Offline-first, not offline-later
- Teacher-first, not platform-first
- Useful before impressive
- Simple flows over many flows
- Grounded output over fluent nonsense

## Failure Modes To Avoid

- building too many user roles
- adding dashboards before core utility
- chasing five prize tracks at once
- making Gemma 4 invisible in the product
- optimizing for polish before usefulness
- pretending a concept video is the same as a working product
- trying to support all Nigerian languages in version one

## Decision Filter

Before adding any feature, ask:

1. Does this help a real teacher prepare or teach better?
2. Can we demo it cleanly in under 20 seconds?
3. Does it strengthen our Future of Education story?
4. Does it showcase Gemma 4 in a concrete way?

If at least three answers are not yes, do not build it now.
